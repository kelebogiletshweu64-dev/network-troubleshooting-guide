Common Network Problems

📡 No Internet Connection

Check your router, restart your device and make sure Wi-Fi is enabled.

🔌 Ethernet Not Working

Check that the Ethernet cable is connected properly and inspect the cable for damage.

🖥️ IP Address Problems

Check your IP configuration and make sure your device receives a valid IP address.

📶 Weak Wi-Fi Signal

Move closer to your router and reduce interference from other devices.

Useful Network Commands

ipconfig

Displays your computer's IP configuration.

ping

Tests connectivity between your computer and another device.

tracert

Shows the route packets take to reach a destination.
